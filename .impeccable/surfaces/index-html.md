# Homepage surface

- **Scope and visitor mode:** Responsive UAE health-insurance marketplace homepage; Persuade.
- **Audience and job:** UAE residents and families want to find and compare health-cover options with less guesswork.
- **Action and task:** Enter household, emirate, age range, and priority; choose a sample comparison lens; compare two or more profiles side by side; create a local demo brief.
- **Proof and content:** A working discovery flow, priority-based sample ordering, and a comparison table demonstrate the marketplace interaction without fabricated carrier or policy data.
- **Constraints:** The page is a front-end prototype. No insurer, policy, premium, eligibility, or quote service is connected; do not imply that a real quote request is sent.
- **Chosen direction and memorable moment:** Swasthya Shuraksha Comparison Exchange; the first viewport previews a comparison ledger, and the same ledger grammar powers profile selection and side-by-side comparison.
- **Unresolved decisions:** Provider partners, policy terms, premium data, licensing, market-specific legal requirements, and production quote submission.
