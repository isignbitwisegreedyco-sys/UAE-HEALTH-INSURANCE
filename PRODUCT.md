# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

UAE residents and families comparing health insurance options and looking for a clear way to request a quote.

## Product Purpose

Help shoppers understand the available kinds of cover, narrow choices around their needs, and take the next step toward a quote.

## Positioning

Swasthya Shuraksha is an original, comparison-led UAE health-insurance marketplace concept. Its Indian-language-inspired name means "health protection." The prototype focuses on helping shoppers compare the questions behind sample cover profiles rather than copying another marketplace's branding or offers. No exclusive market position has been validated.

## Operating Context

The first surface is an English-language, responsive consumer website for UAE shoppers. Its local demo flow asks who needs cover, the emirate, an age range, and a comparison priority; it does not collect contact or medical details.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The initial site is a front-end prototype; no insurer, payment, identity, or quote-provider integrations are available.
- Sample profiles and comparison prompts demonstrate the shopping experience only. No live plan details, premiums, provider ratings, savings, or turnaround times are available.
- Quote-form submission can demonstrate the interaction locally but must not claim to request a real quote.
- Legal, licensing, carrier, and product-coverage details remain undecided.

## Brand Commitments

Use Swasthya Shuraksha as the working brand name for this prototype, with a comparison-led visual identity for UAE health-insurance shoppers. This is an original concept name, not an existing insurer, operating company, trademark clearance, or claim of affiliation. Do not reproduce Policybazaar or other marketplace branding, copy, or proprietary assets.

## Evidence on Hand

No insurer data, product catalog, licensing evidence, customer testimonials, or quote service is available in the repository. Any demonstration content must be identified as sample content.

## Product Principles

- Make it easy to start without asking for unnecessary personal details.
- Help shoppers compare relevant options before asking them to commit.
- Be explicit about what is illustrative and what the prototype can actually do.
