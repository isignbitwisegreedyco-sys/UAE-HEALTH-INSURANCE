04 Good to know

Before you compare.

Are these real health insurance plans?

The three profiles are illustrative comparison lenses. The prototype has no live insurer, policy, premium, eligibility, or quote-service data.

Does this demo send my details anywhere?

Your choices are used for the on-page sample result and are not submitted or saved to a server. The flow does not ask for medical information.

What should I verify before choosing a policy?

Confirm the provider network, co-payments, deductibles, benefit limits, exclusions, eligibility, and full policy wording directly with the insurer or a licensed adviser.
