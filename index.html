Good to know

Questions, answered.

Insurance can be complicated. We’re here to make the important parts easier to understand.

What does “no brokerage added” mean?

We don’t add a brokerage fee or markup to the premium you pay. The insurer sets the final premium and receives your payment directly. Always check the insurer’s quote for the final amount and any applicable charges.

Can I see a confirmed price on this website?

A confirmed premium depends on your circumstances and the insurer’s underwriting. We won’t invent prices: the insurer must confirm your final quote and policy terms before you buy.

Does every plan cover the same things?

No. Benefits, provider networks, exclusions, limits, and eligibility can vary by insurer and plan. Review the policy documents and confirm what’s covered with the insurer before choosing.

Is this site an insurer?

No. ClearCover is a guide to making sense of health insurance options, not an insurer. Any policy is issued by the insurer named in your final quote and policy documents.
